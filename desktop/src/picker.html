Allow Screenpeek to share your screen

macOS asks you to switch this on once. Watching never needs it.

  1. Click Open System Settings below.
  2. Under Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Screenpeek.
  3. Come back and click Relaunch Screenpeek. macOS only applies it after a relaunch.
