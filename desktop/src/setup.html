Screenpeek for Mac

Welcome. Let’s get you set up.

It’s the same Screenpeek, with one thing a browser can’t do.

  • Lasers right on your screen. When you share, everyone’s pointers appear over whatever you’re showing, in any app. Only you see them there.
  • ✓Nothing else changes. Rooms, codes, names and flips work just like on screenpeek.me.
  • ✓Viewers don’t need the app. Anyone can still join from a browser.
Step 1

Move Screenpeek to Applications

Screenpeek is running from your Downloads or the disk image. macOS only remembers its screen recording permission once it lives in your Applications folder.

That didn’t work. In Finder, drag Screenpeek into Applications, then open it from there.

Step 2

Allow screen recording

Needed to share your screen. macOS asks once; if you only watch, you can skip this.

  1. Click Open System Settings.
  2. Under Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Screenpeek. If macOS offers to quit and reopen it, that’s fine.
  3. Come back here: this page notices, and you relaunch Screenpeek once.
Checking…
All set

You’re ready to share.

Start a screenpeek, turn on the laser pointer, and viewers’ lasers show up right on your screen. You can switch that off with On my screen any time.
