Screenpeek for Mac

The laser, right on your screen.

Everything Screenpeek does in the browser, plus one thing a browser can’t: when you share, everyone’s laser pointers appear directly on your screen, over every app.

Download for Mac ↓

Free · Apple silicon and Intel · macOS 12 or later

Installing takes a minute

  1. Open the download and drag Screenpeek onto the Applications folder in the window that appears.
  2. Open Screenpeek from Applications. macOS will say it can’t check the app for malicious software, because Screenpeek isn’t registered with Apple yet. Click Done.
  3. Allow it once: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Screenpeek. Confirm, and it opens. You only do this once.
  4. Follow the short setup. Screenpeek asks for screen recording permission (only needed to share your screen) and walks you through it.

Only watching? You don’t need the app: anyone can join a screenpeek from their browser.
